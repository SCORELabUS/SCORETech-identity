The section-based document presentation and subject structure were adapted from TenTuPlaza's public legal pages; SCORE content concerns central identity only, not examinations, rankings, payments or commercial marketing.

Edit the text and its last-updated date here. Rendering publishes `/identity/legal/terms` and `/identity/legal/privacy` without authentication. No legal publication service, GitHub App, historical URL tree or external document host is needed.
